Decades of Professional Excellence

The Department of Metallurgical Engineering at Government Polytechnic, Visakhapatnam, is dedicated to nurturing skilled metallurgical diploma engineers. The curriculum is highly practical, focusing on hands-on lab experiments in mineral dressing, chemical analysis, microstructures, heat treatments, and mechanical testing.

Our students undergo intensive 6-month industrial internships, gaining real-world experience at prestigious organizations such as Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL), ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel (AMNS), and regional foundry clusters. This industry-embedded model gives our diploma holders an edge in core sector careers.

A Legacy of Technical Education

Government Polytechnic, Visakhapatnam, established in 1956, is one of the oldest and most prestigious technical education institutions in the state of Andhra Pradesh. Operating under the Department of Technical Education, AP, it offers high-quality diploma programs to meet the booming industrial demand of the region.

Located in the heart of the steel city of Visakhapatnam, the campus provides easy access to major manufacturing hubs. The college hosts standard classrooms, large machine shops, student recreation areas, and is adjacent to major transport corridors, making it a hub for student learning.

Laboratory Infrastructure

Equipped with state-of-the-art instruments representing advanced research and testing capabilities.

Physical Metallurgy & Metallography Laboratory

This laboratory is dedicated to preparing metallic specimens and revealing their internal microstructures. Students learn to cut, mount, grind, polish, and chemically etch specimens to identify phases, grain sizes, grain boundaries, and metallurgical defects.

Equipment Details

  • Specimen Cutting Machine: Diamond saw precision cutter.
  • Bakelite Mounting Press: High pressure hot compression unit.
  • Polishing Disc Machines: Double-disc variable speed polishing units.
  • Optical Microscopes: Equipped with high resolution image analyzing software.

Records & Activities

Conducts over 12 academic experiments per semester, including ASTM grain size estimation, cold-working vs annealing analysis, and inclusion rating of steel samples.

Mechanical Testing Laboratory

In this lab, material mechanical properties are evaluated. Students perform tensile, compressive, shear, impact, hardness, fatigue, and creep testing, linking raw mechanical data to core physical microstructures.

Equipment Details

  • Universal Testing Machine (UTM): 100 kN electronic load frame.
  • Hardness Testers: Rockwell, Brinell, and Vickers hardness setups.
  • Charpy/Izod Impact Machine: Pendulum impact tester for toughness.
  • Rotary Fatigue Tester: Cyclic stress failure analyzer.

Experiment Videos & Records

Access digitized experiment procedures and instructional video catalogs for stress-strain curves, tensile elongation, and brittle-ductile transition temperature testing.

Heat Treatment & Pyrometallurgical Laboratory

Students explore structural adjustments driven by heat. Thermal cycles of annealing, normalizing, quenching, and tempering are carried out in high-temperature muffle and tubular furnaces.

Equipment Details

  • Muffle Furnaces: Operates up to 1200°C with digital programmers.
  • Jominy End Quench Setup: Hardness profile assessment for steel alloys.
  • Salt Bath Furnace: Isothermal annealing and heat transfers.
  • Sand Permeability Tester: Sand casting design evaluation.

Safety & Operations

Safety training is compulsory. The facility features comprehensive thermal PPE, advanced overhead exhaust vents, gas sensor shields, and digital cooling setups.

Advanced Characterization Laboratory

Supporting postgraduate studies and Ph.D. research projects, this lab contains equipment for resolving atomic arrays and nanoscale topography.

Equipment Details

  • X-Ray Diffractometer (XRD): Resolves lattice structures and crystallite sizes.
  • Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM): Topography resolving with EDS chemical microanalysis.
  • Microhardness Tester: Vickers indentation profiling for micro-scale components.
  • High-Energy Planetary Ball Mill: Powder synthesis and solid-state alloy formulation.
